Leakage demonstration
  A naive split of the raw 4920 rows puts an exact copy of 100.0% of test rows in the training set
  and gives Random Forest accuracy 100.0%. That number is inflated by duplicates and is NOT reported as model performance.

Caveats
  * Only 61 test rows (about 1-2 per disease): every metric has wide uncertainty.
  * Most rows are subsets of the same disease's symptom pool, i.e. the data looks synthetic;
    high scores do not imply clinical accuracy.
  * Not a medical diagnosis system.
